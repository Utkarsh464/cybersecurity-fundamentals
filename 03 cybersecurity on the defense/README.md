# Cybersecurity: On the Defense

> **Course:** Cybersecurity: On the Defense (IBM SkillsBuild)
> **Part of:** Cybersecurity Fundamentals learning plan — this is Course 03.
> **Status:** 100% COMPLETE (writeup)

"The defense" is the course that answers the questions the offense course deliberately avoided. On the Offense taught me how attacks _work_; On the Defense teaches me what an organization does about it — with money, strategy, and layered controls that prevent, detect, and respond. Where the offense course is about understanding the attacker, this course is about being the organization the attacker has to get past.

This course sits in the order **Intro → Offense → Defense → Future**. Everything in the defense course reuses the vocabulary built in the Intro course (CIA triad, controls, risk) and the offense course (threat actors, kill chain, MITRE ATT&CK) — and it hands that vocabulary to the two courses after it, which examine the job market and the credential.

## Approach

Same learning-log discipline as the other courses: the course decides what a module contains. Where the course touched a topic lightly (for example, cryptography and threat intelligence), I researched the same topic through primary sources — IBM, NIST, CISA, CISA NCSC, and others — to verify the facts and keep the scope to what the course actually taught.

Where a defense topic overlaps with something I have already tried in my own lab work, I link the relevant repository so the notes connect back to hands-on practice.
